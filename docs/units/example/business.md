## Purpose

Give developers a working reference for composing a simple interactive page.

## Supported outcomes

- A supplied greeting label reaches the page through its provider. [C1]
- Users can increment the demonstration count or reset it to zero. [C2] [C3] [C4]
- The page can show or hide details, initially hidden. [C5] [C6]
- The reference page uses English translation resources. [C8]

## Limitations

This demo has no persistence, remote-data UI, or production routing contract. English example resources do not establish a product locale policy. Counter actions are in-memory; the local detail toggle is per hook instance.
